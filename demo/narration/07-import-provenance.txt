The importer queries wind forecasts by publication windows, rather than requesting only the latest available forecast. It downloads daily windows with timeouts, retries, and a resumable local cache. Forecast records are keyed by target time and publication time, so earlier revisions remain available. Actual generation is filtered to wind and retains its original half hour records. An hourly actual is created only when both component half hours exist. The completed snapshot contains seventeen thousand eight hundred and sixty four forecast revisions, one thousand four hundred and eighty eight half hour actuals, and seven hundred and forty four complete hourly actuals. Metadata records the source requests, retrieval timestamps, record counts, and checksums. January refers to decision dates, so delivery correctly extends from the second of January through the first of February.
