Fresh retrieval does not change the information boundary of a historical decision. Publication time must still be at or before its selected cutoff, independently for each target hour. The previous forecast is the previous distinct eligible publication for that target. Both actual half hours are required before averaging their megawatt values. Missing observations remain unavailable, while zero remains a valid measurement. Headline accuracy uses all complete forecast and actual matches. Revision comparisons use the common hours containing both revisions and the actual. Positive hourly errors multiplied by one hour give the overforecast energy in megawatt hours. January decision dates imply delivery from the second of January through the first of February; source retrieval does not widen the product scope.
