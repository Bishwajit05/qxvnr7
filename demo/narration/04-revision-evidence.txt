The important finding is that a newer forecast does not automatically improve every error measure. On these twenty four complete hours, the latest forecast's mean absolute error was seven hundred and eighty two point seven five megawatts. The previous forecast's was seven hundred and thirty six point two nine. That is six point three percent higher error for the newer update. It improved eight hours and worsened sixteen. Yet its positive overforecast energy was lower: about thirteen thousand six hundred and seventeen megawatt hours, compared with fourteen thousand five hundred and forty eight. These measures describe different aspects of the miss. The explanation view exposes the comparison and its sample size, so the reviewer can inspect the evidence. This single case does not establish that older forecasts are generally better, or that forecast revision size predicts future uncertainty.
