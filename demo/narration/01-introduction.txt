Welcome to Wind Decision Replay. This pilot helps an electricity buying team inspect the wind forecast available for a historical next day purchasing decision and measure its performance against recorded generation. Forecasts, actuals, accuracy cards, and revision evidence are visible immediately when the data loads. There is no reveal or unlock step. The starting example is the fifteenth of January twenty twenty six at nine in the morning, for delivery on the sixteenth. The dashboard reads the permitted Elexon APIs and identifies the source and retrieval status. We will explore the customer workflow, then review the architecture and its checks. This walkthrough uses actual application captures and disclosed synthetic narration.
