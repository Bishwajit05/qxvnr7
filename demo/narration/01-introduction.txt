Welcome to Wind Decision Replay. This pilot helps an electricity buying team understand the wind forecast that was available when a next day purchasing decision had to be made, and then assess how that forecast performed. The starting point is a real historical decision, the fifteenth of January twenty twenty six, at nine in the morning, for delivery on the sixteenth. The application deliberately separates the information available before the decision from the outcome observed afterward. This walkthrough uses screenshots captured from the running application, with synthetic narration. We will first explore the customer workflow, and then review the implementation and its checks.
