Revealing the outcome adds the recorded actual generation and the error measures. The actual line is clearly retrospective; it was not information available to the buyer the previous morning. The forecast is hourly, while the source actuals are half hourly. Two complete half hours are averaged to make a comparable hourly observation. For the midnight delivery hour, the selected forecast was seven thousand three hundred and ninety eight megawatts. The actual hourly average was seven thousand three hundred and thirty five point five, giving a positive error of sixty two point five megawatts. The sign matters. Positive means wind was overforecast. This is an electricity generation mismatch, not a calculation of the client's purchasing requirement, because the permitted data contains neither customer demand nor their contracted portfolio.
