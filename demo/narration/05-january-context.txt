The refresh control makes a new request to Elexon's real historical APIs. The status identifies a successful API response, reuse of the short lived API cache, or an explicitly labelled fallback after upstream failure. Its retrieval timestamp describes when the records were obtained, not when a forecast was originally published. January remains the scope after refresh. The monthly view applies the same cutoff to every January decision and reports accuracy and coverage together. At midnight, some forecasts do not cover all delivery hours, so those observations remain missing. Every revision comparison uses identical hours. A failed refresh preserves last good data and reports the failure instead of silently claiming fresh results.
