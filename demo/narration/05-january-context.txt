The January view applies the same cutoff policy to every decision day. At nine in the morning, all seven hundred and forty four delivery hours have complete comparisons. The latest revision had lower daily mean absolute error on sixteen of thirty one comparable days. Month wide mean absolute error was about two thousand and thirty nine megawatts, root mean squared error about two thousand five hundred and five, and signed bias positive one thousand seven hundred and forty eight. The aggregate is calculated from the individual hourly errors. It is not an unweighted average of daily scores. Coverage remains part of the result. At a midnight cutoff, some forecasts do not yet cover the entire delivery day. Those missing hours stay unavailable. The product gives the team a traceable view of forecast performance, without pretending to supply demand forecasts, trading profit, or a calibrated confidence score.
