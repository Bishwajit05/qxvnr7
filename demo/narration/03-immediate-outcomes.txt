The recorded generation line and error measures appear alongside the forecast as soon as the request completes. Forecasts are hourly, while the source actuals are half hourly. Two complete half hours are averaged to make one comparable hourly observation. In the preserved reference example, the midnight forecast was seven thousand three hundred and ninety eight megawatts and the recorded hourly average was seven thousand three hundred and thirty five point five. The positive error was sixty two point five megawatts. Positive means wind was overforecast. This measures a generation mismatch. The permitted datasets contain neither customer demand nor the client's contracted portfolio, so the dashboard does not claim to calculate the exact electricity they should buy.
